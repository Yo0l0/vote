❓ Pokebot FAQ

Here you'll find answers to common questions and available commands!

🎮 Available Commands:

  • /openpack — Open a random Pokémon pack
  • /packs [set] — Open a pack from a specific set
  • /buypack — Buy a Pokémon pack (40 coins)
  • /mysterybox — Open a mystery box for random prizes
  • /grade [code] — Submit a card for grading
  • /checkgrade [code] — Check grading status
  • /fuse [code1] [code2] [code3] — Fuse 3 identical cards for a better condition
  • /swapcodes [code1] [code2] — Swap codes between your cards

🎒 Inventory & Shop:

  • /inventory — View or filter your cards
  • /find [name] — Find cards by name
  • /search [code] — Search for a card by its code
  • /sell — Sell cards using filters
  • /items — View your items
  • /itemshop — View the item shop
  • /buyitem [item] — Buy an item from the shop

💰 Economy:

  • /balance — Check your coin balance
  • /daily — Claim daily reward
  • /give — Gift coins, cards, or tokens to others
  • /vote — Claim 50 coins for voting

🏆 Leaderboards & Stats:

  • /leaderboard — View top players
  • /collection — See how many cards you own per set
  • /missing [set] — See which cards you're missing
  • /quiz — Answer quiz questions for coins & Aura
  • /quizstats — View your quiz performance
  • /cardtrend [name] — Show card market trend
  • /cooldowns — Check your cooldowns

⚔️ Battles & Duels:

  • /duel [opponent] — Challenge another player to a card duel
  • /deck — Manage your 5-card battle deck
  • /duelprofile — View duel stats & most-used card

🌍 Exploration:

  • /journey — Start your Pokémon world journey (BETA)

💸 Marketplace:

  • /shop list [code] [price] — List a card for sale
  • /shop browse — Browse player listings
  • /shop remove [code] — Remove your listed card

⚙️ Utilities:

  • /setchannel [channel] — Set drop channel for holo events
  • /help — Show all available commands
  • /upcoming — View upcoming card set releases
