FAQ

Common questions

How do I get cards?
Run /openpack for a random pack or /packs [set] to pick a set. Packs are on a cooldown — check it with /cooldowns. You can also buy extra packs with /buypack (40 coins) or try a /mysterybox.
How do I earn coins?
Claim /daily, vote for the bot on Top.gg and run /vote to collect 50 coins, answer /quiz questions, and sell cards with /sell or list them on the player marketplace with /shop list.
What are the rarity tiers?
From most to least common: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Promo, Holo, SIR (Special Illustration Rare) and Mythical. Holo, SIR and Mythical cards shimmer in your collection view.
How does grading work?
Submit a card with /grade [code] and check back with /checkgrade [code]. Grades run from 6 to a perfect 10 — the better the card's condition, the higher the ceiling, and graded cards are worth more.
What does fusing do?
/fuse [code1] [code2] [code3] combines three identical cards into one with a better condition. Conditions go Damaged → Light Play → Near Mint → Mint → Pristine.
How do duels work?
Build a 5-card battle deck with /deck, then challenge someone with /duel [opponent]. Attack, defend, heal and use rarity moves. Track your record with /duelprofile.
Why can't I see my cards on the website?
Log in with the same Discord account you play with. The website syncs with the bot every few minutes, so a fresh pull can take a little while to appear.
How do I set up holo events in my server?
A server admin runs /setchannel [channel] to choose where drops and holo events are posted.
How do I delete my data?
Ask in the support server with your Discord user ID. See the Privacy Policy for details.
