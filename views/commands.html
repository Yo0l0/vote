FAQ

Common questions

How do I get cards?
Run /play for your home screen, then /openpack. Packs cost ⚡ energy: you hold up to three, one refills every two hours, and you earn extra from /daily, gym wins, quiz streaks, Pack Peek and /vote. /buypack sells Standard, Premium and Legendary packs for coins, and busy servers get sealed packs dropped in their drop channel.
How do I earn coins?
/daily (streaks pay more), /vote on top.gg every 12 hours, /quiz, /gym wins, set milestones from /checklist, quests and achievements, and selling: /sell for house prices, /market list for whatever another trainer will pay.
What are the rarity tiers?
Common, Uncommon, Rare, Promo, Holo, Ultra Rare and SIR. Ultra Rares are the full arts (ex, GX, V, VMAX, LV.X, Illustration Rares, Shinings); SIR is the special illustrations, rainbows, golds and Gold Stars. Above all of it: ✦ shinies (one card in 5,000, ranked above SIR), and on any card, a 🥇 1st Edition serial or 🌍 World First from a set's first week.
How does grading work?
/grade opens the desk: pick a lane from Super Express (6h) to Economy (5 days); fees and wait times rise when the queue is busy. The card comes back with four sub-grades (centering, corners, edges, surface) on a half-point scale; the lowest one sets the grade, and four straight 10s is a Black Label. /checkgrade cracks the slab in front of your server. Not happy? Crack it and regrade at your own risk, or keep the card raw.
What are 1st Editions?
Every set has a 1st Edition week when it releases. Every copy pulled in those seven days is a 1st Edition, numbered in the order it was pulled, and never printed again; copy #1 of each card is its World First with a gold stamp. 1st Editions sell for 3×, World Firsts for 10×, and they can't be fused or dusted.
What does the forge do? And fusing?
/craft melts spare duplicates into stardust and forges the exact card you're missing from the same set, with boosts, misfires and Perfect Forges. /fuse combines three identical cards into one of better condition: Damaged → Light Play → Near Mint → Mint → Pristine → Flawless.
How do I trade?
/find card: shows every copy of a card and who holds it. Make an offer with coins, cards or both; the owner gets a DM to accept, counter or decline, up to six rounds. Everything is held in escrow so a yes always goes through. /trade is a two-way table, /market is buy-it-now, /auction is a bid war.
Why can't I see my cards on the website?
Log in with the same Discord account you play with. The site syncs with the bot every few minutes, so a fresh pull can take a little while to appear.
How do I set up drops in my server?
An admin runs /setchannel to choose where wild drops land, and /setchannel for:Announcements for update posts and new-set news.
How do I delete my data?
Ask in the support server with your Discord user ID. See the Privacy Policy for details.
