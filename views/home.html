1st Edition & World First

Every card pulled in a set's first seven days is a numbered 1st Edition with the real WOTC stamp, never printed again. Copy #1 is the World First, stamped in gold. 1st Editions sell for 3×, World Firsts for 10×, and a slab prints 1ST EDITION on its label.

✦

Shinies

One card in five thousand is born shiny: the whole card re-toned by the bot (Neon, Noir, Ghost, Chrome, Gold, Void) with a look and a name rolled from its code, so no two are alike. Ranks above SIR, sells for 20×, and the whole server hears about it.

Features

Everything a collector wants

Built like a real card economy: scarcity, condition, grading, a player market, and a server full of people watching every reveal.

Pokébot Grading Co.

Four sub-grades on a half-point scale, the Beckett lowest-sub rule, Black Labels for four straight 10s. Pick a lane from 6 hours to 5 days; fees and wait times move with the queue. Roll case colours (Obsidian Black is 1 in 400) and paint your slab.

Find, offer, trade, auction

/find shows every copy of a card and who holds it. Put coins, cards or both on the table and haggle for six rounds by DM — every deal is escrowed, so a yes always goes through. Or run a bid war with /auction.

Gym leaders & duels

Battle eight AI Gym Leaders solo, read their moves, earn badges, coins and packs. Or /duel another trainer with a card straight from your binder. Graded cards fight harder.

The forge & fusion

Melt spare duplicates into stardust and forge the exact cards your binder is missing, boosts, misfires and Perfect Forges included. Or /fuse three copies into one of better condition, all the way to Flawless.

Checklists, chase & wild drops

Aim every pack at your chase set and watch the Chase Meter fill: five packs earn a free Chase Pack with doubled holo odds. Set milestones pay out automatically. Busy servers get sealed packs dropped in the channel: first to claim keeps it.

Seasons, quests, leaderboards

A monthly race with prizes, daily and weekly quests, achievements with real rewards, a weekly drop champion in every server, and global boards for binders, Gem Mints, 1st Editions and shinies, right here on the site.
