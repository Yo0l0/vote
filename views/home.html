Features

Everything a collector wants

Built like a real trading-card economy: scarcity, condition, grading and a player-driven market.

Pack opening

Rip packs from Base Set, Jungle, Fossil, Team Rocket, Gym Heroes and more on a cooldown. Buy extra packs with coins.

Pro grading

Send cards to the lab with /grade. Pristine cards can hit a perfect 10 — and are worth far more.

Duels

Build a 5-card deck and challenge trainers. Attack, defend, heal and unleash rarity moves to win.

Marketplace

List cards, browse player shops and track price history with /cardtrend to snipe the best deals.

Card fusion

Fuse three identical cards with /fuse to upgrade their condition toward Pristine.

Leaderboards

Compete globally and per-server for coins, aura, top grades and collection size. Quizzes and journeys earn extra.
